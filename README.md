# 🎯 About CareerMind AI

CareerMind AI is built to act as a **personal AI career companion**.

Instead of using separate tools for resumes, interviews, career planning,
skill development, and job discovery, CareerMind brings these workflows into
one intelligent workspace.

The goal is simple:

> **Understand your career goals. Identify what you need. Build a roadmap.
> Prepare smarter. Move forward with confidence.**

---

# ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI Career Assistant
Get personalized career guidance based on your goals, interests, skills,
experience, and target roles.

### 📄 Resume Builder
Create, improve, and tailor professional resumes for specific career
opportunities.

### 🔍 Resume Analysis
Analyze your resume and identify areas that can be improved for clarity,
skills, keywords, and overall presentation.

### 🎤 Interview Preparation
Practice interview questions and receive AI-powered feedback to improve
your technical and behavioral interview performance.

### 🧠 Skill Gap Analysis
Compare your current skills with the requirements of your target role and
identify what you should learn next.

### 💼 Job Matching
Discover career opportunities that better match your skills, interests,
and career goals.

### 📈 Career Roadmaps
Get structured learning and career roadmaps designed around your target
role and current skill level.

### 📚 Courses & Learning
Explore learning paths and resources that help close identified skill gaps.

### 🏢 Company Discovery
Explore companies and understand potential career opportunities.

### 📊 Progress Tracking
Track your learning, preparation, and career-development progress in one
place.
